Rock Music

Rock Music is a genre of popular music that emerged in the 1950s and
is characterized by a strong beat, the use of electric guitars, bass, and
drums, and often focuses on youth-oriented themes and expressive lyrics.

ORIGINS AND REVOLUTION

Rock Music emerged in the 1950s in the United States, evolving from genres like
rock and roll, rhythm and blues, and country music. Early pioneers such as
Chuck Berry, Little Richard, and Elvis Presley defined the genre's energetic
rhythm and rebellious spirit. By the 1960s, rock had diversified, with bands like
The Beatles and The Rolling Stones shaping the British Invasion and popularizing rock globally.
