Jazz Music

Jazz is a genre of music characterized by improvisation, syncopated
rhythms, swing feel, and a blend of African American musical traditions
with European harmonic structures.

ORIGINS OF JAZZ

Jazz originated in New Orleans, Louisiana in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, heavily influenced by
African American musical traditions, including blues, spirituals, work songs, and ragtime. The city’s vibrant
cultural mix, including Caribbean and European elements, helped shape early jazz, fostering improvisation,
syncopated rhythms, and collective participation in ensembles.
