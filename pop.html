Pop Music

Pop music is a popular genre of music designed to appeal to a wide
audience, characterized by catchy melodies, simple lyrics, and a strong
focus on commercial appeal.

HISTORY AND EVOLUTION

Pop music evolved from the mid-20th century, drawing elements from genres such as rock and roll, rhythm
and blues, and electronic music. Notable periods include:

  • 1950s and 1960s: Rise of artists like Elvis Presley and The Beatles, blending rock and pop sensibilities.
  • 1970s and 1980s: Incorporation of disco, funk, and synth-pop, with artists like Madonna and Michael Jackson leading the way.
  • 1990s and 2000s: Emergence of boy bands, girl groups, and crossover pop stars such as Britney Spears and Backstreet Boys.
  • 2010s to present: Pop integrates more electronic dance music (EDM), hip-hop elements, and global influences, highlighted by artists like Taylor Swift, Ed Sheeran, BTS, and BLACKPINK.
